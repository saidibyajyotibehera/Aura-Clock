# ✨ Aura Clock

A beautiful, modern and minimal animated clock wallpaper for your Windows desktop.

Bring your desktop to life with **Aura Clock**, a web-based live wallpaper designed to work with [Lively Wallpaper](https://livelywallpaper.app/).

## 🌐 Live Preview

**[Launch Aura Clock](https://auraclock-lively.base44.app)**

## 🚀 Installation Guide

Follow these simple steps to set up Aura Clock on your desktop.

### Step 1 — Download Lively Wallpaper
Download and install Lively Wallpaper on your PC or laptop:

https://livelywallpaper.app/

### Step 2 — Open Lively Wallpaper
Launch Lively Wallpaper and navigate to **Add Wallpaper**.

### Step 3 — Add Aura Clock
Enter the following URL:

https://auraclock-lively.base44.app

Click **OK** to add the wallpaper.

### Step 4 — Apply Wallpaper
Select **Aura Clock** from your wallpaper library and apply it to your desktop.

## 🎉 Setup Complete!

Enjoy Aura Clock on your desktop!

## 💻 Compatibility

- Windows PC / Laptop
- Lively Wallpaper
- Internet connection required to load the hosted wallpaper
